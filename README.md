# 🌿 BambooTrack Pro

**A smart and simple bamboo business management application.**

BambooTrack Pro is designed to help bamboo traders and business owners manage their daily operations digitally. From tracking bamboo purchases and sales to managing workers, wages, suppliers, payments, and finances, everything can be organised in one place.

## ✨ Features

### 📊 1. Business Dashboard
- Get an overview of your bamboo business.
- View purchase and sales information.
- Monitor business transactions.
- Visualise category-wise data using charts.

### 🛒 2. Bamboo Purchase Management
- Record bamboo purchases.
- Select bamboo quality categories.
- Enter quantities and purchase prices.
- Calculate total purchase amounts automatically.
- Add supplier information and transaction notes.
- View purchase history.

### 💰 3. Bamboo Sales Management
- Record bamboo sales.
- Select bamboo quality grades.
- Enter quantities and selling prices.
- Automatically calculate total sales.
- Add customer details and notes.
- View sales history and totals.

### 🎋 4. Bamboo Quality Categories

Organise bamboo according to five categories:

- A1
- A2 – High Quality
- A3 – Medium Grade
- A4 – Utility Grade
- A5 – Economy Grade

### 👷 5. Worker Management
- Add and manage workers.
- Store worker names and village details.
- Keep track of daily bamboo-cutting work.
- Set custom wage rates.
- Automatically calculate worker wages based on quantity and rate.

### 💵 6. Payments Management
- Maintain worker payment records.
- Track payments and outstanding amounts.
- Keep payment information organised.

### 🏭 7. Supplier Management
- Maintain supplier records.
- Record supplier advances.
- Track supplier balances.
- View purchase and advance history.

### 👛 8. Digital Wallet
- Record money received and money spent.
- Monitor the current wallet balance.
- Maintain a transaction ledger.
- Review individual financial entries.

### 📈 9. Business Analytics
- Visualise purchase and sales data.
- View category-wise transaction charts.
- Monitor business totals and financial activity.

### 🧮 10. Built-in Calculator
A built-in calculator for quick calculations while managing daily business activities.

### 💾 11. Backup & Restore
Your business records are important.

BambooTrack includes:
- Export complete business data as a JSON backup.
- Import previously exported backup files.
- Restore purchase and sales records.
- Restore workers, work logs, payments, suppliers, advances, and wallet records.

**Note:** Importing a backup replaces the existing records on the device.

## 🌐 Offline Support

BambooTrack is designed to work both online and offline.

- Access the application without an active internet connection after its initial successful load.
- Install and use it as an Android APK.
- Store business records locally on the device.
- Receive updated application code from the hosted website when online, provided the APK is configured to load that website.
- Continue using the cached application when offline.

## 🔐 Data & Privacy

BambooTrack stores business information locally on the device.

- Business records remain in the device's local storage.
- Data is not automatically synchronised between devices.
- Regular backups are recommended.
- Keep exported JSON backup files somewhere safe.
- Avoid clearing app data or uninstalling the APK before exporting a backup.

## 🚀 Project Vision

BambooTrack aims to simplify traditional bamboo business management by bringing everyday operations into a single digital platform.

The goal is to reduce manual record-keeping, make calculations easier, organise business information, and help users manage their work more efficiently.
